The ongoing conflict between Ukraine and Russia has extended its reach to the shores of Libya, as a recent Ukrainian drone strike targeted a Russian warship in the port of Benghazi. The attack, which has been officially confirmed by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy as having sunk the Russian corvette "Mercury," resulted in significant damage to the vessel and a subsequent fire at the Libyan naval base.

Tragically, the incident also led to the deaths of two Libyan naval officers, who succumbed to their wounds sustained during the assault. This development underscores the widening geopolitical implications of the conflict, drawing in other nations through proxy involvement or direct impact.

This strike is not an isolated event. It follows closely on the heels of another Ukrainian drone attack on a Russian cargo ship in the eastern Baltic Sea, indicating a broader strategy by Ukraine to target Russian naval assets across different operational theaters. The effectiveness of these drone operations continues to challenge Russian maritime security and strategy.
