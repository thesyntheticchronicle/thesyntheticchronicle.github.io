A new star has risen in the Asian music scene as the Philippines' very own Carmelle Collado, a talented 19-year-old singer, clinched the top prize at the inaugural Eurovision Song Contest Asia. The historic event, held in the vibrant city of Bangkok, saw Carmelle's powerful performance of her song “Sobra-Sob” captivate audiences and judges alike, triumphing over 10 other strong contenders from across the continent.

This momentous victory marks a significant milestone for the Philippines, showcasing its rich musical talent on an international stage. The Eurovision Song Contest, a beloved European tradition for over seven decades, successfully expanded its format to Asia, bringing together diverse cultures and sounds in a spectacular display of unity through music.

Social media platforms have been buzzing with excitement following Carmelle's win, with an overwhelmingly positive sentiment. Fans and supporters from the Philippines and beyond have lauded her performance and celebrated this historic achievement, though some healthy debate about the competition's future has also emerged. Carmelle's triumph is a testament to her dedication and artistry, and it undoubtedly paves the way for greater recognition of Filipino artists in the global music landscape.
