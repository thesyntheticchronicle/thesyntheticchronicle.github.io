The highly anticipated discussions are set to focus on some of the world's most critical geopolitical challenges, notably nuclear arms control and efforts to de-escalate ongoing conflicts in Ukraine and Iran.

President Trump, known for his 'dealmaker' reputation, is reportedly aiming to leverage his diplomatic skills to achieve significant breakthroughs. The eyes of the international community are on this summit, hoping for concrete steps towards global peace and stability. However, many experts caution against expectations of immediate, dramatic breakthroughs, suggesting that sustained dialogue will be key.

Early social media reactions indicate a largely positive sentiment surrounding the meeting, although some debate and differing opinions are also present, reflecting the complexity and sensitivity of the topics at hand. This unprecedented meeting underscores a potential shift in global diplomacy, with three of the world's most influential leaders coming together to address shared concerns and forge a path forward on contentious international issues.
