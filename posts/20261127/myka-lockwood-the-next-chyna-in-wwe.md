A new powerhouse is emerging in WWE's NXT brand, and the wrestling world is buzzing with comparisons to one of the most iconic female competitors in history: Chyna. Myka Lockwood, a rising star of The Vanity Project, has reportedly captivated WWE officials and prominent wrestling journalists Dave Meltzer and Bryan Alvarez, who are touting her as the 'new Chyna,' potentially even more dominant.

The high praise for Lockwood comes after her impressive performance in a recent NXT match where she teamed with her Vanity Project stablemates. What caught the attention of many was Lockwood's seamless execution of power moves on male wrestlers. Meltzer highlighted that unlike Chyna's era, the current NXT roster is more than willing to 'sell' for Lockwood, making her presentation even more impactful than that of the 'Ninth Wonder of the World.'

According to reports, the willingness of male talent to cooperate has allowed WWE to present Lockwood as a legitimate threat capable of mixing it up with anyone on the roster. This dynamic is a significant factor in the strong belief that Lockwood is destined for a dominant run, potentially even against male competitors in the near future. Backstage sentiment is overwhelmingly positive, with many impressed by her work.

Lockwood's ascent is expected to accelerate further, especially with eight NXT stars slated for a main roster call-up following December's John Cena Classic. This shift will likely clear the path for Myka to make an even greater impact in developmental. The social media landscape largely mirrors this excitement, with a predominantly positive reception to the 'new Chyna' comparisons, albeit with some healthy debate.

As Myka Lockwood continues to forge her path, all eyes will be on how she lives up to the lofty expectations and carves out her own legacy in WWE.
