The Philippines proudly celebrates Katrina Anne Johnson, who captivated audiences and judges alike at the prestigious Miss International 2026 pageant held in Tokyo on November 25. Johnson not only secured a coveted spot in the Top 20 but also earned the distinguished title of Miss International Asia-Pacific 2026, a testament to her elegance, intelligence, and charm.

Her journey through the competition was closely followed by many, with social media buzzing with overwhelmingly positive sentiment and discussions surrounding her performance. While the ultimate crown of Miss International 2026 went to the deserving Elizaveta Kuznetsova of Moldova, Johnson's achievement as a continental queen and a Top 20 finalist brought immense pride to the Philippines.

This recognition highlights the Philippines' continued presence and excellence on the global pageant stage, reaffirming the caliber of its representatives. Katrina Anne Johnson's success serves as an inspiration and a moment of national celebration.
