The New Zealand Women's Rugby League team, affectionately known as the Kiwi Ferns, are once again on the precipice of Rugby League World Cup glory, having powered their way into their seventh consecutive final. Their journey to the championship match was cemented with a commanding 32-0 victory over Samoa in the semi-final.

The semi-final clash saw a standout performance from star centre Stacey Waaka, who demonstrated her prowess by scoring an impressive hat-trick, contributing significantly to the Kiwi Ferns' decisive win. This dominant display has set the stage for a highly anticipated final against their long-standing rivals and defending champions, Australia.

Australia, the Jillaroos, earned their spot in the final by overcoming a spirited Fiji team with a 30-14 victory in their own semi-final. The upcoming final will rekindle a fierce rivalry, with Australia having emerged victorious in the last three World Cup finals against New Zealand. This history adds an extra layer of anticipation to the contest, as the Kiwi Ferns will undoubtedly be looking to break Australia's winning streak and reclaim the coveted trophy.

Social media is abuzz with excitement surrounding the impending final. Sentiment is overwhelmingly positive, with fans eagerly discussing the match-up and the potential outcomes. While there's a strong sense of national pride and support for both teams, some debates and discussions are also taking place online, highlighting the passion and engagement of rugby league supporters.
